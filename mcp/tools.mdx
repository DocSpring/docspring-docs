An assistant connected to [DocSpring's MCP server](/docs/mcp/) sees the tools that its access allows. You choose the
access when you sign in: **Read only**, **Read and write** or **Full access**.

Tools that make PDFs or set up templates wait until DocSpring has finished, like the
[synchronous API](/docs/api-guide/synchronous-requests/). When that takes longer than about 50 seconds, the tool
returns the record's ID with the state `processing`, and the assistant checks it again with `get_submission` or
`get_template`.

## Full access

Full access adds the tools that delete things, and the tools for webhooks and email actions.

### Delete

### Webhooks and email actions

An assistant sees and changes all of your webhooks and [email actions](/docs/template-editor/email-actions/), as you
can in DocSpring. `send_test_email` sends only to you. The webhooks and email actions an assistant sets up stay when
you disconnect it.
