## Getting Started

- You can upload PDF templates and configure form fields in our [template editor](/docs/template-editor/introduction/).
- You can also create a template using [HTML and CSS](/docs/html-templates/overview/).
- You can upload a [Word document](/docs/word-templates/overview/) with tags in it, and get a PDF, a Word document or
  both.
- You can lay out a new PDF in the [Page Designer](/docs/page-designer/overview/), with text, tables, images and your
  data on the page.

You can generate documents by [posting data to our API](/docs/api/#tag/submissions/post/templates/{template_id}/submissions), or filling out an online form. We can [automatically generate web forms](/docs/forms/web-forms/) from the fields in your template. You can also [embed these web forms on your own website](/docs/guides/embedded-forms/embedded-web-forms/), and your users can fill out the form to generate a document.

## Synchronous API

The DocSpring API supports both synchronous and asynchronous requests. Send your requests to the synchronous host, as our API clients do:

- Requests to `sync.api.docspring.com` are **synchronous** by default. _(We wait up to 95 seconds for the PDF to be processed before returning a response.)_
  - _Accounts in our EU region use `sync.api-eu.docspring.com`._
- Requests to `api.docspring.com` are **asynchronous** by default. _(Pending PDF is returned immediately.)_
  - _Accounts in our EU region use `api-eu.docspring.com`._

## E-signatures

You can collect legally binding electronic signatures by creating [signature requests](/docs/forms/signature-requests/). Your users can visit a signing form hosted on [docspring.com](https://docspring.com), or you can embed the signing form on your own website.

## Security and HIPAA Compliance

DocSpring has completed a SOC 2 Type II audit and is GDPR and HIPAA compliant.
We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with customers on every paid plan, including Starter.
Contact [support@docspring.com](mailto:support@docspring.com) to request a BAA.
You must sign a BAA with us before submitting any protected health information (PHI) to DocSpring.
