Credits are consumed when DocSpring processes **live**
submissions or combines PDFs. Watermarked test submissions and preview PDFs
are free. Combined PDFs have no test mode: combining consumes credits even with
a test API token or watermarked source PDFs.

## What a job costs

Each PDF is measured in **units**. One unit covers up to:

| Dimension         | One unit   |
| ----------------- | ---------- |
| Pages             | 10 pages   |
| Template PDF size | 10 MiB     |
| Measured CPU time | 20 seconds |
| Output PDF size   | 50 MiB     |

A PDF's size is the **highest** of these, rounded up (not the sum), and at
least 1 unit, so most PDFs are 1 unit. Size is measured in MiB (1,048,576
bytes). Template size applies to PDF-template submissions. Processing time and
template size count where they're measured; memory isn't charged.

Each kind of PDF costs a number of credits per unit:

| PDF                                              | Credits per unit |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| Filled PDF form (a submission to a PDF template) | 20               |
| PDF from an HTML template                        | 4                |
| Combined PDF                                     | 4                |

Two charges are added on top:

- **Source PDFs** ([combined PDFs](/docs/api-guide/combine-pdfs/)): 4 credits
  for each source document. Generating the source PDFs is charged separately.
- **Signers** (e-signatures): 60 credits for each data request whose signer
  is asked to sign or initial, whatever the size of the PDF. A data request asks
  for a signature when its `fields` include a signature or initials field, or,
  without a `fields` list, when the template has a signature or initials field
  that your API request didn't fill in. Data requests that only collect values
  such as text, numbers and dates add nothing. The charge is made with the final
  live PDF; creating requests, recording events, issuing access tokens, and
  generating previews have no separate charge.

Retained PDFs have a separate [storage charge](#stored-pdfs) after their first
30 days.

### AI field setup

[AI field setup](/docs/template-editor/automatic-field-detection/) is priced
per page, in dollars:

- Each account gets **100 free AI pages** every calendar month (UTC).
- After that, each page AI sets up costs **$0.25**. Naming a page's fields and
  setting up its conditional logic count as one page. A run that keeps your
  field names and only sets up conditional logic counts each page it reads.
- The $0.25 is paid from credits you've paid for, at the price you paid for
  them: 100 credits bought at 0.25¢ each, or 50 bought at 0.5¢. Free credits,
  such as the Developer plan's monthly credits, can't pay for AI pages.
- Detecting fields without AI names is free, and so is a page whose AI naming
  fails. Each run counts its pages, including a run that suggests changes for
  you to review; applying the changes you've reviewed is free.
- A run starts only when your free pages and credits cover all of its pages.
  New uploads that can't be paid for still get their fields, with plain names.
- Accounts on legacy plans get the free pages each month, and AI field setup
  stops when they're used.

The [credit usage report](/docs/billing/#credit-usage-reports) lists AI field
setup by day and by template.

### Examples

| Job                                     | Credits |
| --------------------------------------- | ------- |
| A filled PDF form of up to 10 pages     | 20      |
| A 45-page filled PDF form               | 100     |
| A 3-page PDF from an HTML template      | 4       |
| A 3-page filled PDF form with 2 signers | 140     |
| Combining 12 one-page PDFs              | 56      |

Combining 12 one-page PDFs costs 48 credits for the source PDFs plus 8 credits
for the 12-page combined PDF (2 units). What a credit costs depends on your plan
or credit pack: the [pricing page](https://docspring.com/pricing/#pdf-prices)
shows the price of each kind of PDF on every plan and pack.

## Stored PDFs

Each completed PDF includes **30 days of storage**, starting when it becomes
available. After that, storage costs the greater of:

- **1 credit per 50 MiB-month** of stored PDFs; or
- **1 credit per 50 PDF-months**.

Both components are prorated for the time PDFs are stored during the UTC
calendar month. We take the higher component, then round up **once for your
account for that month**. We do not add the two components or round each PDF
separately. A nonzero monthly total can therefore cost one credit even if you
store less than 50 MiB and fewer than 50 PDFs.

For example, after the included 30 days:

| Storage retained for a whole month | Monthly credits |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------- |
| 1,000 PDFs totalling 500 MiB       | 20              |
| 1,000 PDFs totalling 5 GiB         | 103             |
| 250,000 PDFs totalling 100 GiB     | 5,000           |

Retaining 1,000 PDFs totalling 500 MiB for half a month costs 10 credits. PDFs
expired within their first 30 days have no storage charge.

The same rates and included period apply on **every plan, including Developer**.
Generated PDFs and combined PDFs are measured as separate retained files. A
combined PDF's source submissions count separately while you keep them. Normal
watermarked test submissions, previews, templates and temporary processing files
have no storage charge. Combining has no test mode, so retaining a combined PDF
can incur storage charges even when its sources were watermarked.

Storage charges are collected from your credit balance after the month ends,
independently of your subscription renewal date. Developer accounts use the same
schedule without needing a Stripe subscription. Spending credits on storage does
not create another payment invoice.

### If storage credits are due

If your unreserved balance cannot cover the whole storage charge, we keep your
PDFs and show the credits due in Billing. We email your billing email address and give
you **seven days** to add credits before new live PDF jobs pause. Already admitted
jobs can finish; downloads, expiration controls, watermarked test PDFs and previews
remain available. Funding pays outstanding storage charges before admitting more
live work, and waiting submissions resume automatically.

Automatic top-up can fund storage charges when enabled, using your chosen pack
and spending cap. Storage never enables automatic top-up for you. An unpaid
storage charge can trigger an opted-in purchase even above your usual threshold.

Storage continues to accrue during the grace period and while live jobs are
paused. **Expire PDFs you no longer need to stop future storage charges.**
Expiration does not erase charges for storage already used. Cancelling a paid
subscription does not turn off storage billing or automatic top-up.

### If storage stays unpaid

We don't keep PDFs indefinitely while their storage is unpaid. If storage
credits are still due **90 days** after live jobs paused, we delete your stored
PDFs that are past their included 30 days of storage, together with their
submission data. Your account, templates and newer PDFs are not affected, and
the storage charge already owed stays due until you add credits.

We email your billing email address **30 days** and again **7 days** before the
deletion date, which is also shown in Billing. Adding enough credits to pay the
storage charge at any point before then cancels the deletion.

## Credit expiry and rollover

- **Monthly plan credits** (Starter, Growth, Scale) roll over for **12
  months**, so occasional busy months are covered by quieter ones.
- **Purchased credit packs** also expire after **12 months**.
- **Developer plan credits** (the free monthly grant) don't roll over. They
  reset with each monthly grant.

Credits are always spent **oldest expiry first**. The **Billing** page shows the
next credits to expire, and its Usage tab lists every upcoming expiry date.
When at least 200 credits from a subscription, credit pack, plan upgrade or
courtesy grant will expire within 30 days, we email your billing email address
once about them. Free Developer credits are not included because they reset every
month.

## Low-credit warnings

When your balance drops below the greater of **20% of your monthly allowance or
100 credits**, we queue an email to your billing email address immediately. We send one
warning while the balance stays low. Adding enough credits resets the warning
for the next drop. If your balance recovers before the email is sent, we skip it.

Warnings are suppressed while automatic top-up can recover the balance. If
top-up has reached its spending cap or requires your attention, low-credit
warnings remain enabled.

## What happens when you run out

When your balance can't cover a live job (at least one unit of it, plus 60
credits for each signer or 4 for each source PDF), or storage credits are
overdue:

- New **live** PDF generation pauses. API requests return
  `402 Payment Required` and the submission is saved in the
  `insufficient_credits` state, and nothing is lost.
- Paused submissions show as **Waiting for Credits** and resume automatically
  once enough credits are available (buy a pack, enable auto top-up, or receive
  your next monthly grant). A submission resumes once your balance covers at
  least one unit of it, plus 60 credits for each signer (or 4 credits for each
  source PDF of a combined PDF); a larger PDF can need more. A PDF that has
  already rendered becomes available once your balance covers it.
- Work waits up to **30 days** from when it first ran out of credits. After
  that it isn't processed: the dashboard shows **Not processed** and the API
  reports the `error` state. You can discard waiting work with **Discard
  waiting submissions** on the **Billing** page, or from a submission's page.
- No webhook is sent when work starts waiting. When it expires or is discarded,
  DocSpring sends the `submission.failed` (or `combined_submission.failed`)
  webhook.
- **Test** submissions keep working, so development is never interrupted.

See [Credits & Rate Limits in the API guide](/docs/api-guide/credits-and-rate-limits/)
for how to handle these responses in your integration, and
[Credit Packs & Auto Top-ups](/docs/billing/credit-packs-and-auto-topup/) for
keeping your balance topped up automatically.
