DocSpring uses a prepaid credit system. Every plan includes a monthly credit
allowance, and generating documents consumes credits based on the work
involved. This page explains the plans; see
[How Credits Work](/docs/billing/how-credits-work/) for the credit cost model.

:::note[Plans purchased before prepaid credits]
Accounts on a subscription plan purchased before prepaid credits were introduced
keep their existing pricing, monthly PDF allowance, and API rate limits. The
credit rules described here apply to the current plans. To move to a current
plan, contact [support@docspring.com](mailto:support@docspring.com).
:::

## Plans

Plans differ in monthly credits, API rate limits, support level, and a few
features:

| Feature                                   | Developer          | Starter             | Growth                | Scale                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Templates                                 | 10                 | 25                  | Unlimited             | Unlimited                |
| Users                                     | 2                  | 10                  | Unlimited             | Unlimited                |
| Signature requests                        | Yes                | Yes                 | Yes                   | Yes                      |
| Embedded template editor                  | No                 | Yes                 | Yes                   | Yes                      |
| Hosted forms                              | DocSpring branding | Unbranded           | Unbranded             | Unbranded                |
| Embedded forms, HTML templates, test PDFs | Yes                | Yes                 | Yes                   | Yes                      |
| HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA)  | No                 | Yes                 | Yes                   | Yes                      |
| Unused credits                            | Reset monthly      | Roll over 12 months | Roll over 12 months   | Roll over 12 months      |
| Support                                   | Documentation      | Email & chat        | Priority email & chat | Slack channel & priority |

Pending invitations count toward the user limit.

If your account moves to a plan with lower limits (for example after a
downgrade), your existing templates and users keep working; you can't add more
until you're within the new limits. API requests for a feature your plan
doesn't include return `403 Forbidden` with `"code": "upgrade_required"`.

| Plan          | Monthly credits      | Live API rate limit | Test API rate limit |
| ------------- | -------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| **Developer** | 200 (free)           | 10/min (burst 20)   | 5/min (burst 15)    |
| **Starter**   | 10,000 or 20,000     | 60/min (burst 120)  | 5/min (burst 15)    |
| **Growth**    | 50,000 or 100,000    | 120/min (burst 240) | 5/min (burst 15)    |
| **Scale**     | 200,000 – 10,000,000 | 300/min (burst 600) | 5/min (burst 15)    |

See [What each PDF costs](https://docspring.com/pricing/#pdf-prices) for each
plan's and pack's price per PDF.
Each paid plan offers a choice of credit tiers, so you can pick the monthly
allowance that matches your volume.

:::tip[The free Developer plan is for building]
Integrations can take weeks or months to build. On the Developer plan you can
build and test your integration in test mode, then subscribe when
you go live. Watermarked test submissions and preview PDFs don't consume
credits. Combining PDFs consumes credits even with a test API token or
watermarked source PDFs; combined jobs have no test mode.
:::

## Starting out

You start on the free Developer plan with 200 credits per month, enough for 10
filled PDF forms. The plan has no time limit.

You can try a paid plan's features (more templates and team members, live data
requests and e-signatures, the embedded template editor and unbranded hosted
forms) free for 14 days, without a card. A trial keeps the Developer plan's
credits. It starts when you choose a paid plan at signup, or from **Choose a
plan** on the **Billing** page. Each account gets one trial, and an account
that has had a paid plan can't start one. When it
ends, your account has the Developer plan's features again: nothing is deleted,
and templates or team members over its limits keep working, but you can't add
more until you subscribe.

When you're ready for more credits, subscribe to a paid plan from the
**Billing** page (only the account owner can subscribe): add a card, then
select **Choose a plan** and pick your plan and credit tier. Your subscription
starts once the first payment succeeds. If the payment needs card
verification, complete it using the payment link; your free plan remains active
until then. If you chose a paid plan at signup, the billing
page shows a prompt to finish setting it up whenever you're ready.

Your subscription renews monthly and can be changed or cancelled at any time. If
you cancel, or if a renewal payment ultimately fails, your account moves back
to the free Developer plan. Nothing is deleted and your account is never
suspended.

### Nonprofits

Registered nonprofits and charities get the Starter plan free, with no card.
Email [support@docspring.com](mailto:support@docspring.com) from your account's
email address with proof of your status. See
[DocSpring for nonprofits](https://docspring.com/nonprofits) for who qualifies
and what to include.

## Changing plans

You can change your plan or credit tier at any time with **Change plan** on the
**Billing** page:

- **Upgrades** take effect once payment succeeds. You're charged a prorated amount for
  the remainder of the billing period, and a matching prorated share of the
  credit difference between your old and new tier is added to your balance as
  soon as the payment succeeds. The new tier's full allowance starts at your
  next renewal. A declined payment or pending card verification leaves your
  current plan active.
- **Downgrades** apply the lower plan's limits and support immediately, with no
  partial refund. The lower price and monthly credit allowance take effect from
  your next billing cycle, and credits you've already received keep their
  original expiry.
- **Cancelling** (under **Billing settings**) keeps your paid plan active until the end of the current
  billing period, then moves your account to the free Developer plan. Your
  unexpired credits stay in your account. Automatic top-up is controlled
  separately and remains enabled if you have opted in.
- Choosing another plan removes a scheduled cancellation, even if the upgrade
  payment still needs verification. Use **Keep subscription** to undo cancellation
  without changing your plan.

## Managing billing

The **Billing** page has four tabs:

- **Overview**: your credit balance and next expiry, your plan and its limits,
  automatic top-up and your payment method. Buy
  [credit packs](/docs/billing/credit-packs-and-auto-topup/), change your plan or
  set up [automatic top-ups](/docs/billing/credit-packs-and-auto-topup/#automatic-top-ups)
  from here.
- **Usage**: the monthly [credit usage report](#credit-usage-reports), upcoming
  credit expiry dates and stored PDFs.
- **Invoices**: every payment, with links to view or download each invoice.
- **Billing settings**: payment methods, your business billing address and
  VAT / tax ID for invoices, your billing email (the address that receives
  invoices and billing emails; the account owner's email unless you set
  another), cancelling your subscription, and deleting your account. To stop
  paying without losing anything, cancel your subscription: your account moves
  to the free Developer plan and keeps its templates, PDFs and unexpired
  credits. Deleting your account removes access and forfeits unused credits.

Problems that need attention, such as a failed payment, an unpaid purchase or
work waiting for credits, appear at the top of every tab and on your dashboard.
Team members can see the Overview and Usage tabs; only the account owner can
make changes.

## Stored PDFs

Every PDF includes 30 days of storage. After that, monthly storage costs the
greater of 1 credit per 50 MiB-month or 1 credit per 50 PDF-months, prorated for
time stored and rounded up once per account per UTC calendar month. These rates
also apply on Developer. Storage uses your credit balance independently of
subscription renewals.

## Credit usage reports

Open the **Usage** tab on the **Billing** page (also linked from your dashboard) to
see monthly credit usage.
Choose a month to view daily totals, generation, merging, signature request and storage
breakdowns, usage by template, and individual charge history. Download the
selected activity as CSV. Months and charge timestamps use UTC.

The account credit statement shows allowances, pack purchases, consumption,
expiry, refunds and adjustments separately. It explains changes in credits;
invoices remain your payment history. Reports update in the background and
show when recent activity is still being added.

Storage details show the month the storage was used and any amount still due.
A storage debit appears in credit activity when those credits are actually
collected, which can be in the following month. An unpaid assessment is not
counted as credits already spent.

Stored-PDF totals update daily. Credit activity updates in the background as
credits are added or spent; recent activity can take a short time to appear.
