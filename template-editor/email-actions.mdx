An email action emails each submission's files to the people you choose, as soon as the submission is ready: its PDF,
and a [Word template](/docs/word-templates/overview/)'s Word document and OpenDocument file. It can send to fixed
addresses, such as your team's, to the address someone typed into one of the template's fields, and to an address in
the submission's metadata. Each email has your subject and message, and attaches the files or links to them. It can
send for every submission, or only for the submissions that meet its conditions.

Add an email action to one template, or to your account to send it for
[every template, or the templates and folders you choose](#email-actions-for-every-template).

Email actions email anyone on a paid plan (Starter or higher). On the Developer plan or during a free trial, you can set
them up and try them out, but they only email your team: **Send a test**, and test submissions' emails to your team
members. Live submissions' emails aren't sent until the account has a paid plan.

## Adding an email action

In the template editor, click **Settings**, open the **Emails** tab and click **Add email action**. A template can have
several, such as one for the person who filled in the form and one for your records.

- **To**, **Cc** and **Bcc**: addresses, and the [addresses each submission gives](#addresses-from-the-submission): pick
  a text or email field, or type `metadata.` and a key. In **To**, pick **Each signer** to send each of the submission's
  [signers](#signature-requests) their own email. An email can have up to 10 recipients, counting each field and
  metadata value as one.
- **From name**: the sender's name. Leave it blank to use your account's name.
- **From address**: an address at one of your [verified email domains](#sending-from-your-own-domain). Leave it blank
  to send from DocSpring's address.
- **Reply-To**: where replies go, a team member's address or one at your verified email domains. It starts as your own
  address.
- **Subject** and **Message**: the email's text, with [variables](#variables). Leave them blank for a short default
  that names the template.
- **File name**: the attached files' name, with [variables](#variables), such as `Lease {{ data.unit }}`. Each file
  gets its own extension: `.pdf`, `.docx` or `.odt`. Leave it blank for the name in the submission's `pdf_filename`
  metadata, or the template's name. A download link's files keep the names their other downloads have.
- **Format**: on a Word template, which of the submission's files to send (see [below](#word-templates)).
- **Delivery**: attach the files, or **Send a download link** (see [below](#attachment-or-download-link)).
- **Submissions**: live submissions, test submissions, or both. Test submissions only email your team members'
  addresses, so you can try the action out without emailing anyone else.
- **Conditions**: **Every submission**, or
  [only the submissions that meet these conditions](#sending-only-some-submissions-emails).

Email actions belong to the template's draft and take effect when you save them. Publishing a version doesn't change
them.

**Send a test** emails the action's email to you only. It shows each field's value as the field's name in brackets, and
each metadata value as `[metadata.key]`, so no submission's data goes anywhere. When the action attaches its files and
the template is a PDF template, the test attaches the template's blank PDF. Otherwise the test has no attachment, and
says what the real emails have. Without a paid plan, it needs your email address to be confirmed.

## Email actions for every template

To send the same email for many templates, add it to your account: open **Integrations** in the sidebar, click **Set up
email actions** on the **Email** card, then **Add email action**. It has the same settings as a template's email
action, and:

- **Sends for**: **Every template**, or **Chosen templates and folders**: the templates you pick (type a name or an
  ID to find one) and the templates in the folders you pick, including the folders inside them. You can pick up to 500
  templates and 500 folders. To send for more templates than that, put them in a folder and send for the folder.
- In **To**, **Cc** and **Bcc**, type a field's name to send to its value. A template without the field skips it, a
  `{{ data.field_name }}` variable for a field that a template doesn't have prints nothing, and a condition on one
  reads it as blank.
- Use `{{ template.name }}` in the subject or message to say which template the files are from.
- **Format** can choose Word documents or OpenDocument files. Only Word templates make them, so the action sends no
  email for the other templates' submissions.

**Send a test** sends the email as it would go for the template it sends for that you changed most recently.

### Changing one for a template

A template's **Emails** tab lists your account's email actions that send for it, under **From your account**:

- The switch turns one off or on for this template only. One that's off for your account shows **Off**, and is off for
  every template.
- **Customize** changes its settings for this template only. The settings you don't change follow your account's email
  action, so later changes to them there apply here too. Each changed setting is marked **Changed for this template**:
  **Reset to the account's setting** undoes one, and **Reset all** undoes every change.
- **Edit it for every template** opens your account's email actions.

Deleting one of your account's email actions also deletes its changes on every template.

## Addresses from the submission

**To**, **Cc** and **Bcc** can each send to an address that every submission gives:

- **A field**: pick one of the template's text or email fields. Each submission's value gets the email. For a field in
  a list, every row's address gets it.
- **Metadata**: type `metadata.` and the key, such as `metadata.agent_email`, or `metadata.agent.email` for a value
  inside an object (up to 5 levels deep). Submissions get their metadata from the
  [API](/docs/api/#tag/submissions/post/templates/{template_id}/submissions) or a web form link's
  [`metadata` parameters](/docs/forms/web-forms/#set-submission-metadata).

A field or metadata value left blank, or without a valid address, is skipped for that submission, and the submission
page says why. A field that a [conditional logic](/docs/template-editor/conditional-logic/) rule hides gets no email:
the PDF doesn't show it either.

To send to an address the PDF shouldn't print, such as the agent who sent the form, either put it in the submission's
metadata, or add an email field and check **Hidden** in its settings. A hidden field is on the online form and in the
API, but not on the PDF, and its value still gets the email. Fill it in with the API, or hide it on the form too with a
web form link's [`hidden_fields` and `data` parameters](/docs/forms/web-forms/#hidden-fields).

With the API, these are tags in `to`, `cc` and `bcc`: `{{ data.client_email }}`, `{{ data["client email"] }}`,
`{{ data["tenants/~/email"] }}` for a field in a list, or `{{ metadata.agent_email }}`.

## Sending only some submissions' emails

Choose **Only the submissions that meet these conditions** under **Conditions**, then add the conditions, as in a
[conditional logic](/docs/template-editor/conditional-logic/) rule: a field, a condition (such as **Equals**, **Is one
of** or **Greater**) and a value. With more than one, choose whether all of them or any of them must match. An
email action can have up to 20 conditions.

Conditions read the values the PDF shows: formulas are worked out, and a field that a rule hides counts as blank. Number
comparisons need a number field, and conditions can't check a field in a list. A submission that doesn't meet them sends
no email, and its page shows **Skipped** with **Conditions not met**. A test email always sends, and says that the
action has conditions.

With the API, set `send_when` to `{ "match": "all", "conditions": [...] }`, with conditions in the format of a
template's conditional logic rules.

## Variables

The subject, message and file name can include these variables:

| Variable                                      | Value                                      |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `{{ data.field_name }}`                       | A field's value, as the PDF shows it       |
| `{{ data["field name"] }}`                    | The value of a field whose name has spaces |
| `{{ metadata.key }}`                          | A value in the submission's metadata       |
| `{{ template.name }}` and `{{ template.id }}` | The template's name and ID                 |
| `{{ submission.id }}`                         | The submission's ID                        |
| `{{ account.name }}`                          | Your account's name                        |

The `if`, `unless` and `case` tags work too:

```liquid
Hi {{ data.first_name }},

Your signed lease for {{ data.address }} is attached.
{% if data.pet_deposit != blank %}
Your pet deposit is {{ data.pet_deposit }}.
{% endif %}
```

**Insert a field** puts a field's variable where your cursor is. A variable that isn't one of these, or a field that
isn't on the template, is an error when you save. A field you remove later prints nothing.

## Word templates

A Word template's submissions make Word documents, OpenDocument files (.odt) or PDFs, as its **Output** setting
chooses, or the formats an API request asks for (see [Output Format](/docs/word-templates/overview/#output-format)).
Choose which of them an email action sends under **Format**:

- **All files** _(the default)_: every file the submission made.
- **Word document (.docx)**, **OpenDocument file (.odt)** or **PDF**: only that file.

A submission that didn't make the chosen file sends no email, and its page shows **Skipped**, with **No Word document to
send** for example. A file the submission makes later, such as a PDF made from its Word document, isn't emailed. With
the API, set `file_formats` to `docx`, `odt`, `pdf` or `all`.

## Attachment or download link

- **Attach the PDF** (or **Attach the files**) attaches the submission's files, with the audit trail when the template
  merges it into the PDF. Files over the action's attachment size limit, together, go as a download link instead.
- **Send a download link** sends a link to a DocSpring page with a button to download each file. Each link works for 7
  days. After that, the page lets anyone the email went to ask for a new link, which is emailed to them. Links stop
  working once the submission's files are deleted (see
  [Expire Submissions](/docs/template-editor/settings/#expire-submissions)).

Accounts that have signed a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with us start with download links.

### Attachment size limit

Emailing a file makes it about 38% larger, and many company mail servers refuse emails over 10 MB. So when an action
attaches its files, files over 7 MB together go as a download link instead: 7 MB of files makes an email of about 9.6
MB.

If everyone the action emails accepts larger emails, such as Microsoft 365 (up to 36 MB) or Gmail (50 MB), raise the
limit with the slider under **Delivery**, up to 25 MB. A template can change it for its own emails. With the
API, set `max_attachment_mb`. 1 MB is 1,000,000 bytes. See
[Email Attachment Size Limits](/docs/guides/email-attachment-size-limits/) for the limits of each mail service.

On DocSpring Enterprise, emails go through your install's own email service, so its limit applies too: Postmark, for
example, accepts emails up to 10 MB, which fits about 7 MB of files.

## Sending from your own domain

Emails come from DocSpring's address with your account's name, and replies go to the action's Reply-To address:

```
From: Acme Inc via DocSpring <no-reply@notify.docspring.com>
```

To send from your own address instead, verify your domain:

1. Open **Email domains** in the sidebar and click **Add domain**. Use the domain you'll send from, such as
   `example.com`, or a subdomain such as `mail.example.com`.
2. Add the DNS records it shows where you manage your domain's DNS (your domain registrar or DNS host):
   - three **DKIM** CNAME records, which sign the emails (required),
   - a **MAIL FROM** MX and TXT record, which let SPF pass for your domain too (recommended),
   - a **DMARC** TXT record, if your domain doesn't have one yet (required).
3. DocSpring checks for the records every few minutes, and the domain is verified once its DKIM and DMARC records are
   found. DNS changes can take a few hours to show. Click **Check again** to check now.

Then choose an address at the domain in an email action's **From address**. Keep the records in your DNS: we check them
every day, and email actions can't send from a domain whose records are gone.

If the DKIM records aren't found within 3 days, the domain needs new ones: click **Check again** to get them, then
replace the three CNAME records.

Enterprise installs send email actions through the email service they're set up with, and have no Email domains page.

## Failures and the submission page

The **Integrations** tab of a submission's page lists each email's recipients and what happened to their email:
**Sent**, **Delivered**, **Not delivered** (the recipient's mail server kept refusing it, for example because the
mailbox was full), **Bounced**, **Marked as spam**, or why it wasn't sent (a field left blank, an address that isn't
valid, a test submission, an address listed twice, which gets one email, or a signer who gets their own email).

Some failures mean the action's settings need fixing: the From address's domain is no longer verified, or a field it
sends to was renamed or removed from the template (the **Emails** tab shows **Field missing**). When an action's emails
keep failing, the account owner gets an email (unless you turn off **Email the account owner when its emails fail**),
and again a day later. After 3 days of failures the action turns itself off. Fix the problem, then turn **Send
emails** back on.

Without a paid plan, live submissions' emails aren't sent. The submission page says why, and they don't count as
failures.

A fixed address (in **To**, **Cc** or **Bcc**) that bounces or marks an email as spam turns the action off straight
away, since every later email to it would fail too, and the account owner gets an email. When a template's changes to
one of your account's email actions added that address, only that template's email turns off.

Problems with an address that came from a submission's field, its metadata or its signers (left blank, not an address,
or bounced) are shown on the submission but don't turn the action off.

## Signature requests

Choose **Each signer** in **To** to send the PDF to the people who signed the submission's
[signature requests](/docs/api-guide/create-signature-requests/). Each signer gets their own email, addressed only to
them, so signers don't see each other's addresses. Everyone else in **To**, **Cc** and **Bcc** gets one email together,
which leaves out anyone who already gets their own as a signer. With the API, it's `{{ signer.email }}` in `to`.

When DocSpring emails a submission's [signature requests](/docs/api-guide/create-signature-requests/#emails-from-docspring),
it also emails the signed PDF to the signers, the CCs and the notification addresses once everyone has signed. An email
action that sends the PDF to one of those addresses replaces DocSpring's email there, so nobody gets the PDF twice: an
email action to **Each signer** replaces DocSpring's emails to the signers.

## API

Email actions and email domains have API endpoints, and the API clients have methods for them. A template's email
actions include your account's email actions that send for it, with the template's `customization` of each, which
you change or remove with its own endpoints. Your account's email actions are at `/email_actions`. A submission's
`actions` include its email actions' emails.

An AI assistant connected to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp/) with full access can set up and change email actions too.
