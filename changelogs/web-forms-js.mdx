Web Forms JS embeds a template's web form on your own website. People fill in the form's fields, and the
submission generates a PDF.

<Aside type="note" title="Versions 2.x">
  Web Forms JS 3.0.0 is a new web form, with a new function,
  `DocSpring.createWebForm()`, and new file names. Versions 2.x stay at
  `https://cdn.docspring.com/embed/simple_form.v<version>.js` and `.css`, and
  pages that load them keep working. To upgrade, see [Upgrading from
  2.x](#upgrading-from-2x).
</Aside>

## Changelog

### 3.2.0 <span class="ml-2.5 relative -top-0.5"><Badge text="Latest" variant="success" size="small"></Badge></span>

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Nov 13, 2026
</div>

- **Option labels:** a field's options can have labels, which the form's radio
  buttons and select lists show in place of the options themselves (Married
  filing jointly for `married_filing_jointly`). The success screen's variables
  show the label too, and the submission's data keeps the option. Set them up
  under **Option labels** in the template editor's field options. An option
  without a label shows in sentence case when the field's options are all
  lowercase words joined by underscores (Head of household for
  `head_of_household`). See [Field
  Options](/docs/reference/field-data-types/#field-options).
- A text field says when what's typed won't fit in its box on the PDF, and how
  many characters to take out ("Too long for this box: take out 3
  characters."), as it's typed. The form measures the text in the font, size
  and box the PDF prints it in, shrunk as far as the PDF would shrink it.
  Before, the PDF cut off what didn't fit. The form can't be submitted until
  the text fits. Text the form starts with (a default or your page's
  `defaultData`) isn't checked, and neither is a field that stays editable in
  the PDF, since its text scrolls, a field that a rule hides, or text of more
  than 5,000 characters.
- A field with **Print digits only**, whose comb boxes print only the value's
  digits, takes any punctuation between them (`123-45-6789`,
  `+1 (555) 123-4567`), and says how many digits it takes when the value
  doesn't fit ("Enter no more than 9 digits, and no letters."), instead of
  "This isn't in the format this field needs."
- A typed date needs all four digits of its year, as the field's example shows
  it: `02.28.20` is no longer read as 2020, and the field asks for a date. A
  date is also read with its year first (`2020/02/28`), with dashes around a
  month's name (`28-Feb-2020`) and with the day of the week before it.
- A number typed with a `$` or with commas between its thousands (`$25.00`,
  `1,250.50`) is read as that number. Before, the field dropped the decimal
  point as it was typed, so `$25.00` became `$2500`. A comma can be the
  decimal mark, as many countries write it: `25,50` is 25.5 and `1.250,50` is
  1250.5 (a comma with exactly three digits after it still separates thousands,
  so `1,250` is 1250). A whole-number field reads a comma only between
  thousands: `1,00` there is more often a mistyped `1,000` than 1.00, so the
  field asks for a whole number. On a phone, a number field opens the number
  pad only when its minimum is 0 or more, since an iPhone's number pad has no
  minus sign.
- A checkbox's description shows under its label. Before, it showed above the
  box, where in a list of checkboxes it read as the one above's.
- A group whose fields a rule hides leaves no gap where it was.
- When a required field's label wraps, its `*` stays with the label's last
  word. Before, on a narrow screen, it could sit alone on the next line.
- A field or group without a title is named after its name with a letter at
  the end capitalized: `list_a` is "List A", where it was "List a". (This
  comes from the template's JSON schema, so every version shows it.)

### 3.1.1

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Nov 8, 2026
</div>

- **Ticks in the list of sections:** a section gets its tick once people press
  **Next** or the submit button on it, or enter an answer in it. Opening a
  section only to look at it no longer ticks it, even when nothing in it is
  required.
- **Signature fields work in live forms:** people can sign a live submission,
  not just a test one. A form with a signature field no longer says at the top
  that it can't be signed, and its signature pads, **Next** and submit buttons
  stay on. Forms on 3.0.0 and 3.1.0 sign live submissions too, without an
  upgrade. See [Signature
  fields](/docs/forms/js-libraries/web-forms-js/#signature-fields).

### 3.1.0

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Nov 7, 2026
</div>

- **Open any section:** people can now open any section from the list of
  sections, to see what's coming or to fill sections out in any order. Leaving a
  section this way doesn't check it. **Next** still checks the section they're
  on, and the submit button still checks the whole form. To keep later sections
  locked until people reach them with **Next**, turn on the template's **Fill
  out sections in order** setting, or use the new `sectionsInOrder: true` option.
  See [Sections](/docs/forms/js-libraries/web-forms-js/#sections).

### 3.0.0

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Nov 4, 2026
</div>

A new web form, built on React instead of jQuery and Alpaca. It loads from
`https://cdn.docspring.com/embed/web_form.v3.0.0.js` and `.css`, and you create
it with `DocSpring.createWebForm()`. It takes the same options and sends the same
data as 2.x. Pages that load 2.x keep working until you upgrade them.

- **Sections:** a template's web form can show its fields in sections, one at
  a time, with Back and Next buttons and a list of the sections beside the form
  (above it on a narrow screen, or at any width with the new `sectionList: "top"`
  option). Set them up with **Sections** in the template editor. See
  [Sections](/docs/forms/web-forms/#sections).
- **Success screen:** once the form is submitted, its place on your page shows
  a title, the template's message, the PDF (when submissions are public) and a
  **Fill out again** button. Set them in the template's Forms settings, or with
  the new `successTitle`, `successMessage` and `showFillAgainButton` options.
  `showSuccessScreen: false` keeps the form, as 2.x did. A form that redirects
  doesn't show it. See [Success
  screen](/docs/forms/js-libraries/web-forms-js/#success-screen).
- **Variables:** the success screen's title and message, and the redirect URL
  after its `?` or `#`, can show what the person submitted, with variables such
  as `{{ data.first_name }}`. `successTitle` and `successMessage` can also be
  functions that return the text. See
  [Variables](/docs/forms/web-forms/#variables).
- **Image fields:** an **Add an image** button opens a dialog where the visitor
  chooses, drops or pastes an image file, or gives its web address, and the
  field shows the image. Files are resized in the browser to at most 2,400
  pixels on their longest side. See [Image
  fields](/docs/forms/js-libraries/web-forms-js/#image-fields).
- **Errors at each field:** errors show under the fields they're about as soon
  as the visitor leaves a field, and for every field when they submit the form
  or try to leave a section. An error goes as soon as the visitor fixes it, and
  a new one waits until they leave the field. Errors DocSpring finds in the data
  show at their fields too.
- **Signature fields:** signatures come only from [signature
  requests](/docs/forms/js-libraries/signature-requests-js/), which record who
  signed and when. A form with a signature or initials field says at the top
  that it can't be signed as soon as it loads, and its signature pads, Next and
  submit buttons are turned off. On your template's hosted form, the **Test**
  switch turns the pads on for test submissions. To print an image or a name on
  the PDF, use an Image or Text field. See [Signature
  fields](/docs/forms/js-libraries/web-forms-js/#signature-fields).
- **Styling:** set the form's colors, font, corners and spacing with CSS custom
  properties, in your CSS or with the new `theme` option, and give it a dark
  theme with the new `darkMode` option. See [Styling web
  forms](/docs/forms/js-libraries/web-forms-js/#styling-web-forms).
- **Strict Content Security Policies:** the form works when `style-src` has no
  `'unsafe-inline'`, without a `style-src-attr` exception, and its stylesheet
  loads no fonts. The date picker's one `<style>` element carries the nonce of
  the `<script>` tag that loads Web Forms JS.
- **No jQuery:** the library no longer includes jQuery, so it leaves your page's
  `jQuery` and `$` alone, and it no longer sets `Handlebars`, `moment` or
  `Alpaca`. Like Visual Forms JS, it includes polyfills for JavaScript features
  a browser lacks. See
  [Globals](/docs/forms/js-libraries/content-security-policy/#globals).
- **Dates** are typed or pasted into the field, in the template's date format or
  as `2026-10-30` or `Oct 30, 2026`, or picked from the calendar that opens under
  it, which has lists for the month and year.
- When the form can't be sent, it says what went wrong above its buttons, as well
  as calling `onError`.
- The form saves what people type under `docspring-web-form:<template ID>` in
  `localStorage`, so a web form and a visual form for the same template no
  longer overwrite each other's saved values. Values that 2.x saved under the
  template ID move to the new key the first time the form loads.
- **Fixes:** a currency field shows its default value instead of NaN, and takes
  negative amounts. Fields whose names contain `&`, `>` or `\` are no longer
  left out of the form. When conditional logic hides every field in a group, the
  group's box is hidden too, instead of showing its title with nothing in it.
  Fields can be called `constructor`, `toString` or any other name.

#### Upgrading from 2.x

1. Load the 3.0.0 stylesheet and script instead of `simple_form`:

   ```html
   <link
     rel="stylesheet"
     href="https://cdn.docspring.com/embed/web_form.v3.0.0.css"
   />
   <script src="https://cdn.docspring.com/embed/web_form.v3.0.0.js"></script>
   ```

   [DocSpring Enterprise](/docs/enterprise/) serves them at
   `/assets/embed/web_form.css` and `/assets/embed/web_form.js` on your
   DocSpring domain. Enterprise no longer serves `/assets/embed/simple_form.js`
   or `simple_form.css`.

2. Rename `DocSpring.createSimpleForm()` to `DocSpring.createWebForm()`. The
   arguments and options are the same. In 3.0.0, `createSimpleForm` throws an
   error that gives the new name. If your page calls `DocSpring.createForm()`,
   2.x's first name for the same function, rename it too: 3.0.0 doesn't define
   it, and Visual Forms JS uses that name for its own function.
   `DocSpring.SIMPLE_FORM_VERSION` is now `DocSpring.WEB_FORM_VERSION`.
3. Put the form's element on the page before you call `createWebForm`: it looks
   for the element straight away, where 2.x looked once the template had loaded,
   and it throws an error when nothing on the page matches the selector. The
   first argument can also be the element itself.
4. If your `processTemplateSchema` callback returns a value, return the schema,
   or nothing. 3.0.0 uses an object it returns as the schema; 2.x ignored it.
5. If your `onError` callback reads the error, update it. Every error is now an
   object with an `error` code and a `message`:

   | Error                            | 2.x                                                       | 3.0.0                                                                                                             |
   | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | The form couldn't be saved       | jQuery's response: `status`, `statusText`, `responseJSON` | `error: 'invalid_data'` when DocSpring rejects the data, otherwise `'failed_request'`, with `status` and `errors` |
   | The template couldn't be loaded  | `error: 'failed_request'`, with `response`                | `error: 'failed_request'`, with `status`                                                                          |
   | A request for the PDF failed     | `error: 'failed_request'`, with `response`                | `error: 'failed_request'`, with `status`                                                                          |
   | The PDF failed, or took too long | `invalid_status` or `submission_timeout`                  | No change                                                                                                         |

6. Update your CSS. The form draws its own `.dsp-web-form` element inside your
   container, with classes that start with `dsp-`. The `.alpaca-*` classes are
   gone, except on the buttons: the submit and Clear buttons keep
   `alpaca-form-button-submit` and `alpaca-form-button-clear`. For colors, fonts
   and sizes, set the [custom
   properties](/docs/forms/js-libraries/web-forms-js/#styling-web-forms) instead
   of overriding the form's rules.
7. If your page used the jQuery that 2.x added to it, load your own copy.
8. If your `onSave` or `onProcessed` callback shows your own confirmation, pass
   `showSuccessScreen: false` to keep the form on the page, as 2.x did, or remove
   your message and let the success screen show the template's.
9. If your page removes the form's saved values before it creates the form (when
   one browser fills in the form for different people or records), remove
   `docspring-web-form:<template ID>` instead of the template ID:
   `localStorage.removeItem("docspring-web-form:tpl_123456")`. 3.0.0 moves 2.x's
   saved values to that key the first time it loads, so removing the old key no
   longer clears them.
10. Your Content Security Policy no longer needs `style-src-attr 'unsafe-inline'`
    for the web form, and the web form's stylesheet loads nothing from Google
    Fonts. If your `style-src` has no `'unsafe-inline'`, give the `<script>` tag
    that loads Web Forms JS a nonce it allows: a form with a date field adds one
    `<style>` element, which carries that nonce.

### 2.7.3

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Oct 29, 2026
</div>

- No longer logs a Content-Security-Policy error in Chrome on a page whose
  `script-src` doesn't allow `'unsafe-inline'`. The jQuery it includes tested
  for an old Internet Explorer event by setting an `onfocusin` attribute, which
  the policy blocked, and the page sent a report for it if it collects them.

### 2.7.2

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Oct 27, 2026
</div>

- Fixed forms for templates with an initials field, which failed to load with
  "Unable to find field class for type: initials". Initials fields now show a
  pad to draw initials, like signature fields. Earlier versions load these
  forms again too, with a signature pad for each initials field.
- A date the date picker rejects no longer reaches your page's
  `window.onerror` handler, so your error tracker no longer reports it as a
  JavaScript error.

### 2.7.1

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Oct 27, 2026
</div>

- New icons, drawn by the form's own stylesheet: the form no longer loads an
  icon font, so your page's Content Security Policy doesn't need to allow
  fonts from our CDN.
- Fixed the icons in date fields: the calendar button and the date picker's
  arrows didn't show on pages that don't load Font Awesome themselves, and the
  time, today and clear buttons never showed.
- In a field with a date and a time, the date picker's button for the time now
  says what it does ("Set the time", and "Back to the date").
- The asterisk on a required field and the placeholder text are darker, so
  they're easier to read.

### 2.7.0

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Oct 27, 2026
</div>

- [Conditional logic](/docs/template-editor/conditional-logic/) now runs on the
  same rule engine as visual forms, signature requests and PDF generation, so a
  condition matches the same values everywhere.
- Number conditions read a field's value as a number only when it's a decimal
  number, such as `5`, `-1.5` or `1e3`: a value like `0x10` isn't 16.

### 2.6.3

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Oct 18, 2026
</div>

- When the form redirects after the PDF is processed, it now waits one second
  after calling `onProcessed`, so the callback has time to finish (for example,
  an async request) before the page changes. The Submit button stays disabled
  until the redirect.
- Fixed the `submission_timeout` error message, which said "60000 seconds"
  instead of "60 seconds".

### 2.6.2

<div class="mt-2.5 text-sm text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400">
  Released on Oct 7, 2026

- Fixed radio buttons and checkboxes losing their selection when an option value
